# Stella Constellation Figures

Twelve transparent, bright-blue constellation diagrams matching the approved
preview. Generated with `node scripts/generate-stella-constellations.mjs`.

Star positions: HYG v4.1, David Nash / Astronomy Nexus, CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database

Constellation connections: d3-celestial, Copyright 2015 Olaf Frohn, BSD-3-Clause.
https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial

The projected figures are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Full source license
notices are included alongside these images. Coordinates use a north-up gnomonic
projection of the project's existing J2000 catalog; these are constellation
figures, not zodiac-symbol illustrations.
